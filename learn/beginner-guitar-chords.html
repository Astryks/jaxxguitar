The 4 beginner guitar chords: G, D, Em and C

If you are new to guitar, learn G, D, Em and C first. Together they make the G - D - Em - C loop, which is the I - V - vi - IV progression in G major. That pattern sits under a huge number of pop songs, which is why it is Lesson 1 in Jaxx Guitar.

ChordFrets (low E to high e, x = don't play)
G3 2 0 0 0 3
Dx x 0 2 3 2
Em0 2 2 0 0 0
Cx 3 2 0 1 0

How to practise them

  1. Tune your guitar first (see how to tune a guitar).
  2. Press just behind the fret with your fingertips, not on top of it.
  3. Strum once on each chord, then switch. Speed comes later.
  4. Loop G - D - Em - C slowly until the changes feel easy.
