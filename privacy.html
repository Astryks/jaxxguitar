Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-07.

The short version

Jaxx Guitar has no account system, no server, and no database. It runs entirely on your device. There's nothing to sign up for, nothing stored about you on a server, and nothing sold.

What's stored, and where

Your lesson progress, streak, XP, stars, review schedule, learned songs and settings (like left-handed mode) are kept in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device, isn't tied to your identity, and can be erased by clearing site data for this website - or, in the iPhone/iPad app, by deleting the app.

Microphone

We use your phone's microphone only to hear your guitar strings, nothing else, and only when you turn it on: for the tuner and for Wait for me / Play in time practice, which listen for the note you play. Audio is analyzed live on your device and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. You can decline microphone access and still use everything else (you can tap the on-screen fretboard instead).

Uploading your own recording

If you upload an audio/video file so Jaxx Guitar can work out its chords, the file is processed entirely on your device by an open-source model (Spotify's Basic Pitch) that comes with the app. The file is never uploaded anywhere.

"Which song is this?" (iPhone and iPad app)

When you upload a song in the iPhone/iPad app and tap Guess the song, the app uses Apple's ShazamKit. It turns about 12 seconds of the recording into an audio fingerprint on your device and sends only that fingerprint to Apple to look the song up, never the recording itself. We don't receive or store it. Apple's privacy policy applies to that lookup. The website doesn't do this.

Tip jar (iPhone and iPad app)

Tips are optional and handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details, and a tip unlocks no features. The app only remembers on your device that you left one, to say thank you.

MIDI

In browsers that support Web MIDI, a connected MIDI guitar/controller's notes are used live on your device only - never recorded or sent anywhere.

Network requests

The app's code, song data, sounds (made on your device), photos and the note-finding model all come with the app. Apart from Guess the song (above), only two optional things reach another company:

  • Cover art: a song's page asks Apple's public iTunes Search for its album cover, sending only the song's title and artist (nothing about you). The picture is saved on your device, so it's only asked for once. Tapping it opens the song in Apple Music.
  • Videos: nothing loads from YouTube until you tap a Watch button. On the website the video then plays from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com); in the iPhone/iPad app it opens in YouTube. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to what you watch there.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, logins or passwords.
  • No analytics, trackers, advertising or ad SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data - there's none to share.
  • No tracking cookies.

Children's privacy

Jaxx Guitar collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated to match.
