Jaxx Guitar

Learn any song on guitar, free.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-06. Plain language, and accurate to exactly what this app does.

The short version

Jaxx Guitar has no account system, no server, and no database. It runs entirely on your device. There's nothing to sign up for, nothing stored about you on a server, and nothing sold.

What's stored, and where

Your lesson progress, streak, XP, stars, review schedule and learned songs are kept in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device, isn't tied to your identity, and can be erased by clearing site data for this website — or, in the iPhone/iPad app, by deleting the app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you turn it on, for the tuner and for Wait for me / Play in time practice, which listen for the note you play. Audio is analyzed live on your device and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. You can decline microphone access and still use everything else (you can tap the on-screen fretboard instead).

Uploading your own recording

If you upload an audio/video file so Jaxx Guitar can work out its chords, the file is processed entirely on your device by an open-source machine-learning model (Spotify's basic-pitch) bundled inside the app. The file is never uploaded anywhere.

MIDI

In browsers that support Web MIDI, a connected MIDI guitar/controller's notes are used live on your device only — never recorded or sent anywhere.

Network requests

None to third parties. The app's code, song data, sounds (synthesized on your device) and the transcription model are all bundled with the app.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, logins or passwords.
  • No analytics, trackers, advertising or ad SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there's none to share.
  • No tracking cookies.

Children's privacy

Jaxx Guitar collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated to match.
