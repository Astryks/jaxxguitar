## Description (1695/4000 chars)

Learn guitar for free with Jaxx the beagle, from your very first chord to full songs.
65+ guided lessons, 270 songs with chord diagrams, and a built-in mic tuner, all in one app.
No ads. No account. No paywall. Just pick up your acoustic or electric guitar and play.

WHY JAXX GUITAR
Most guitar apps lock the good stuff behind a subscription. Jaxx Guitar is completely free. If you love it, there's an optional tip jar, and that's it.

LEARN STEP BY STEP
• 65+ lessons that take you from the basics to barre chords and solos
• Teacher videos that show you exactly what your hands should do
• Daily review to keep what you've learned fresh
• Streaks to help you build a practice habit

PLAY 270 SONGS
• Chord diagrams for every song
• Capo and key info so you can play along in the right place
• Solo cards for lead parts
• Upload any song and get its chords, worked out on your device

FALLING NOTES ON A FRETBOARD
Watch notes fall onto a fretboard and play along in three modes:
• Listen: hear how it should sound first
• Wait for me: listens to your guitar and waits until you play the right note
• Play in time: keep up with the beat once you're ready

TUNE UP IN SECONDS
The mic tuner listens to your guitar and supports Standard, Drop D, DADGAD and Open G tunings.

TRAIN YOUR EAR AND YOUR TIMING
• Chord Ear Gym: learn to recognise chords by sound
• Built-in metronome for steady strumming and practice

MADE FOR EVERY PLAYER
• Left-handed mode flips the fretboard and diagrams
• Works for acoustic and electric guitar
• No sign-up, so you can start in seconds

Jaxx Guitar is made by Astryks. Free, no ads, no account, with an optional tip jar if you'd like to support development.

More at jaxxguitar.com
