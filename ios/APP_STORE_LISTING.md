## Review notes
No login. The microphone is optional (tuner, Wait for me, Play in time) and only hears the user's guitar; audio is analysed on the device and never recorded or sent. Everything also works by tapping the on-screen fretboard. "Upload any song" transcribes a user's own recording on the device; the optional "Guess the song" button uses ShazamKit (an audio signature only). Song pages show chord progressions only, album art from the iTunes Search API, and "Watch" buttons that open official videos on YouTube. The optional Tip jar (bottom of the home screen, collapsed by default) offers three consumable In-App Purchases (com.jaxxguitar.app.tip.small / .medium / .large). Tips are donations: they unlock no content or features, and everything in the app stays free.

## App Privacy
Answer "No, we do not collect data from this app." Nothing is collected by us. Three things leave the device, all disclosed in the privacy policy:
- **Cover art:** the song title and artist go to Apple's iTunes Search.
- **Guess the song (optional):** an audio fingerprint, not the audio itself, goes to Apple's ShazamKit.
- **Videos:** these open on YouTube only when tapped.

## Content rights
**"Does your app contain, show, or access third-party content?"** Answer Yes: chord progressions, album art through the iTunes Search API, and links to official YouTube videos. Before answering "Do you have the rights?", Sid should confirm these points:
