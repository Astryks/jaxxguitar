## Description
Jaxx Guitar is a free, friendly guitar course that runs entirely on your device — no account, no ads, no tracking.

START FROM ZERO
• Choosing a guitar, its parts, the six strings, tuning, and how to press a fret without buzzing
• Lesson 1: G, D, Em and C — the four chords behind hundreds of songs
• Strumming patterns, smooth chord changes, the capo, reading tab, power chords, barre chords and the 12-bar blues

FALLING NOTES ON A REAL FRETBOARD
• Notes fall onto the exact string and fret to play, colored by string
• Listen — hear it played; Wait for me — it waits until it hears you through the microphone; Play in time — scored, with stars

GO FURTHER
• Scales: minor pentatonic, major, natural and harmonic minor, blues — in any key and position
• Solo studies: what Hotel California and November Rain are built from
• Fingerpicking, CAGED and modes

SONGS
• 169 songs with each one's easiest capo position and chord diagrams, plus a play-along with a drum beat
• Optional World songs in 10 languages
• Upload a song you own: Jaxx Guitar works out the chords on your device

TUNER, METRONOME & HABITS
• Per-string tuner that turns green when you're in tune
• Metronome with tap tempo
• XP, levels, daily quests, streaks and a 2-minute daily review
• Fun facts for curious kids: how guitars are built, why they ring, and legendary players

Chord names and progressions only — no lyrics or copyrighted transcriptions.
