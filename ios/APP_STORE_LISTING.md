## Description (updated 2026-10-07)
Jaxx Guitar is a free, friendly guitar course with Jaxx the kitten as your guide. Learn real songs from your very first lesson.

In about a minute you'll learn G, D, Em and C: the four chords behind more than 100 popular songs.

WHAT YOU GET, FREE
• 60+ lessons: tuning, your first chords, strumming, changing chords, the capo, tab, power chords, barre chords, 7th chords and the 12-bar blues, scales, fingerpicking, CAGED, modes, and solo studies
• Genres: pop, rock, blues, jazz, reggae and classical, each with its own chords and strum
• Looping: play rhythm and lead with a friend, or build songs with a loop pedal like Ed Sheeran
• 180+ songs in a Netflix-style library, sorted by genre, with album covers. Tap a song and the chords fall onto the fretboard straight away
• Every song shows its key, its chords section by section, the easiest capo position and chord diagrams, with the official video to listen along
• Falling notes on a real fretboard: Listen, Wait for me (it waits until it hears your guitar), or Play in time with stars. We use your phone's microphone only to hear your guitar strings, nothing else
• Upload any song: record a song you love and Jaxx works out the chords on your device
• Chord Ear Gym: train your ears to name all 24 major and minor chords
• Per-string tuner, metronome, streaks, a 2-minute daily review, and fun facts about guitars and the people who made them

Chord names and progressions only. No lyrics or copyrighted transcriptions. No account, no ads; your progress stays on your device.

## Review notes
No login. The microphone is optional (tuner, Wait for me, Play in time) and only hears the user's guitar; audio is analysed on the device and never recorded or sent. Everything also works by tapping the on-screen fretboard. "Upload any song" transcribes a user's own recording on the device; the optional "Guess the song" button uses ShazamKit (an audio signature only). Song pages show chord progressions only, album art from the iTunes Search API, and "Watch" buttons that open official videos on YouTube.

## App Privacy
Answer "No, we do not collect data from this app." Nothing is collected by us. Three things leave the device, all disclosed in the privacy policy:
- **Cover art:** the song title and artist go to Apple's iTunes Search.
- **Guess the song (optional):** an audio fingerprint, not the audio itself, goes to Apple's ShazamKit.
- **Videos:** these open on YouTube only when tapped.
