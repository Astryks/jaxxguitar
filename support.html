Common questions

Is Jaxx Guitar free? Yes. Every lesson, song and tool is free, with no ads and no account.

Where are my lessons and progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

No sound? Turn the volume up, then tap any key or button once: after a call, a video or another app, the first tap wakes the sound again.

The microphone doesn't hear my guitar. Allow microphone access in Settings > Jaxx Guitar, and play a little louder, close to the phone.

Where is the Tip jar? In the iPhone and iPad app: open the Lessons tab (the home screen), scroll to the bottom and tap Tip jar. Tips are optional and don't unlock anything.

Uploading a song: choose an audio or video file from your device (or record one). Everything is worked out on your device; nothing is uploaded to a server.
