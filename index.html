Frequently asked questions

Is Jaxx Guitar free?

Yes. Jaxx Guitar is completely free, with no ads, no subscription and no paywall. There is an optional tip jar in the iPhone and iPad app if you want to support development, and a tip unlocks nothing extra.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, so you can start in seconds. Your progress and streaks stay on your own device.

What are the best beginner guitar chords?

Start with G, D, Em and C. Jaxx Guitar's first lesson teaches these four chords and the G - D - Em - C loop, the I - V - vi - IV pattern behind a huge number of pop songs.

Does Jaxx Guitar have a tuner?

Yes. The mic tuner listens to your guitar and supports Standard, Drop D, DADGAD and Open G tunings.

Does it work for left-handed players?

Yes. Left-handed mode flips the fretboard and the chord diagrams.

Does it work for acoustic and electric guitar?

Yes. Jaxx Guitar works for both acoustic and electric guitar.

Is my microphone audio recorded or uploaded?

No. The microphone is optional and the sound is analysed on your device. It is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Do the song pages include lyrics?

No. Song pages show the key and chord names only, never lyrics.
